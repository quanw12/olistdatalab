# Help — Evidence and applications

Keep this closed until the timed/independent attempt ends. Reading an approach first makes the attempt guided practice; record that honestly.

<details>
<summary>First hint</summary>

Which concrete behavior can a reviewer reproduce from your project?

</details>

<details>
<summary>Evidence fields</summary>

Write your own project bullet using problem, behavior, verification and scope.
Include links to actual checks or sanitized evidence. Avoid claims about scale or
production ownership that you have not demonstrated.
Check student-year eligibility, location and work schedule separately from skills.

</details>

<details>
<summary>After your first draft</summary>

Read it aloud, answer one follow-up without notes, and remove unsupported claims.
Use current role pages for applications; a still-visible page may have an expired
deadline. Optional platform study can continue after suitable core gates pass.

</details>
