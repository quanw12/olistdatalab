# Readiness, CV evidence and internship applications

## Understand it

A course completion count is not a hiring qualification. Roles vary in required stack, student year, schedule and English expectations. Match evidence to three to five target postings. Explain decisions and limits in plain English. Your projects can show learning initiative, but do not describe them as production employment.

## Syntax and trace

~~~text
Requirement | Required/preferred | My evidence | Missing | Next action
Python      | required           | tested CLI  | ...     | ...
~~~

One posting's preferred Spark exposure does not make Spark mandatory for every DE internship.
