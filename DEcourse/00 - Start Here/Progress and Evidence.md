## What the columns mean

- **Read:** you finished the theory notes and module guide, and answered the review questions.
- **Built myself:** you wrote the code from an empty file (AI allowed for hints, not for the whole solution).
- **Verified:** you have hand-computed expected values, or an independent check, and at least one bad-data case.
- **Can explain:** you can explain the design, the trade-off you chose and its limits to someone else without notes.

## Per-module self-check

- [ ] I made my own attempt before opening any reference.
- [ ] I have a hand-computed expected result or an independent comparison.
- [ ] I tested at least one bad-data or failure situation.
- [ ] I can explain why I chose this approach and where it stops working.
- [ ] I rebuilt part of it from an empty file after 7 days.
